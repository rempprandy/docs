## The six quantitative execution strategies

BlackQuant's Signal Engine runs six specialized quantitative strategies. Rather than issuing a single opaque confidence score, every strategy decomposes market state into weighted, auditable components that determine directional conviction, entry boundaries, and risk parameters.

### 1. Orderflow (`orderflow`)

The **Orderflow** strategy reconstructs the order book's aggressor side by evaluating real-time taker buy and sell volume splits over rolling 5-bar windows. Unlike standard candle feeds that discard aggressor direction, this strategy isolates who is initiating market orders.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * **BUY**: Taker buy volume ratio exceeds 62% of aggregate volume.
  * **SELL**: Taker buy volume ratio falls below 38% (taker sell volume exceeds 62%).
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **Taker Imbalance (Weight: 4)**: Scaled from 62% to 85% aggressor volume dominance.
  * **Participation Rate (Weight: 3)**: Recent trade count compared to a 20-period simple moving average (0.8x to 2.2x normal participation). Imbalance on low trade count is treated as noise; imbalance on heavy trade count reflects true institutional aggression.
  * **Price Confirmation (Weight: 2)**: 5-bar linear price slope agreeing with the orderflow direction. Heavy buying that fails to lift price signals passive institutional absorption (limit-order walls), dampening confidence.
  * **RSI Headroom (Weight: 1)**: Verifies the asset is not severely overbought or oversold prior to entry.
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 1.6x ATR; stop-loss at 1.0x ATR; 45-bar expiration window; 6-minute cooldown.

### 2. Momentum (`momentum`)

The **Momentum** strategy captures directional trend continuation by combining fast-slow exponential moving average crossovers with multi-timeframe macro confirmation and volume acceleration.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * **BUY**: 9-period EMA crosses above the 21-period EMA on the base timeframe, confirmed by a positive slope on the 50-period EMA on the higher timeframe.
  * **SELL**: 9-period EMA crosses below the 21-period EMA on the base timeframe, confirmed by a negative slope on the 50-period EMA on the higher timeframe.
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **Higher Timeframe Alignment (Weight: 3)**: Directional slope of the 50-period EMA on the higher timeframe. Eliminates counter-trend whipsaws.
  * **Volume Expansion (Weight: 3)**: Current bar volume relative to its 20-period average (0.8x to 2.5x volume expansion required).
  * **EMA Separation (Weight: 2)**: Absolute distance between EMA 9 and EMA 21 normalized by ATR (0.05 to 0.50 ATR spread).
  * **RSI Headroom (Weight: 2)**: Verifies RSI is below 75 for BUY signals or above 25 for SELL signals to avoid entering at immediate cyclical exhaustion points.
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 2.4x ATR; stop-loss at 1.4x ATR; 90-bar expiration window; 8-minute cooldown.

### 3. Reversion (`reversion`)

The **Reversion** strategy is a statistical mean-reversion algorithm designed to capture price exhaustion outside historical volatility envelopes. It identifies temporary liquidity vacuums and fades statistical extremes.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * **BUY**: Bar closes beneath the lower Bollinger Band (20-period, 2.2 standard deviations) with 14-period RSI falling below 32.
  * **SELL**: Bar closes above the upper Bollinger Band (20-period, 2.2 standard deviations) with 14-period RSI rising above 68.
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **Band Penetration (Weight: 3)**: Distance of the close beyond the 2.2-sigma band normalized by ATR (0.02 to 0.60 ATR). Deeper extensions signify higher statistical rebound probabilities.
  * **RSI Exhaustion (Weight: 3)**: Severity of the momentum extreme below 32 or above 68.
  * **Capitulation Volume (Weight: 2)**: Volume spike (1.0x to 3.0x average) marking climax absorption.
  * **Anti-Trend Risk Filter (Weight: 2)**: Evaluates the 20-bar slope. If the market is locked in a runaway one-way trend, the fade score is penalized to prevent counter-trend traps.
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 1.8x ATR (targeting mean reversion to the 20-period midline); stop-loss at 1.2x ATR; 60-bar expiration window; 10-minute cooldown.

### 4. Breakout (`breakout`)

The **Breakout** strategy detects directional expansion as price breaks out of multi-session consolidation ranges defined by Donchian channels, verified by volatility expansion.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * **BUY**: Bar closes above the 20-period Donchian Channel High.
  * **SELL**: Bar closes below the 20-period Donchian Channel Low.
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **Breakout Magnitude (Weight: 3)**: Margin cleared beyond the Donchian boundary in ATR multiples (0.02 to 0.70 ATR).
  * **ATR Expansion Percentile (Weight: 3)**: Percentile rank of current ATR over a 40-bar baseline. Breakouts on compressing volatility are filtered out as low-probability false breaks.
  * **Breakout Volume (Weight: 2)**: 1.0x to 3.0x volume surge relative to the 20-period volume SMA.
  * **Extreme Bar Close (Weight: 2)**: Closes within the upper 20% (BUY) or lower 20% (SELL) of the candle's total high-low span, demonstrating directional control through the close.
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 3.0x ATR; stop-loss at 1.5x ATR; 48-bar expiration window; 20-minute cooldown.

### 5. Squeeze (`squeeze`)

The **Squeeze** strategy identifies periods where market volatility compresses to historical extremes, followed by explosive directional releases.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * Prior bar's Bollinger Bandwidth fell into the lowest 25th percentile of historical width over a 40-bar lookback window (the compression phase).
  * Current bar's bandwidth expands (width exceeds prior width), with price closing above the 20-period mid-line for **BUY**, or below the mid-line for **SELL**.
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **Squeeze Tightness (Weight: 3)**: Rank of the compression within the bottom quartile (tighter compression correlates with larger potential releases).
  * **Expansion Velocity (Weight: 3)**: Ratio of current bandwidth to prior bandwidth (1.02x to 1.40x expansion rate).
  * **Directional Conviction (Weight: 2)**: Decisive candle close near the extreme in the direction of the release.
  * **Release Volume (Weight: 2)**: 1.0x to 2.5x volume expansion confirming market participation.
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 2.8x ATR; stop-loss at 1.4x ATR; 40-bar expiration window; 25-minute cooldown.

### 6. Divergence (`divergence`)

The **Divergence** strategy detects structural exhaustion by identifying discrepancies between price action and momentum over a 20-bar lookback window.

* **Directional Triggers**:
  * **Bullish Divergence (BUY)**: Recent price low is lower than the prior price low, but recent RSI low is at least 3 points higher than the prior RSI low.
  * **Bearish Divergence (SELL)**: Recent price high is higher than the prior price high, but recent RSI high is at least 3 points lower than the prior RSI high.
* **Confidence Components**:
  * **RSI Delta Gap (Weight: 4)**: Magnitude of divergence between price and oscillator (3 to 18 RSI points).
  * **Price Extreme Prominence (Weight: 2)**: Depth of the new price high or low normalized by ATR (0.05 to 1.0 ATR).
  * **RSI Turnaround Zone (Weight: 2)**: Evaluates whether the divergence formed within genuine oversold or overbought boundary zones.
  * **Reversal Bar Confirmation (Weight: 2)**: Requires the trigger candle to close in the direction of the expected reversal (bullish bar for BUY, bearish bar for SELL).
* **Risk Boundaries**: Take-profit at 2.6x ATR; stop-loss at 1.5x ATR; 40-bar expiration window; 30-minute cooldown.
