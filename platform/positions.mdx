## Smart Executor execution suites

BlackQuant provides two dedicated operational views depending on your executor's strategy:

### 1. Active Arbitrage Suite (Liquidity Optimizer & MEV Arbitrage)
* **Liquidity Optimizer**: Automated liquidity rebalancing and range arbitrage across decentralized exchange pools.
* **MEV Arbitrage**: Our senior high-frequency trading (HFT) strategy designed for professional market participants, capturing cross-market price discrepancies and optimal transaction routing opportunities.
* **Delegated Non-Custodial Architecture**: Operates via scoped session keys (ERC-4337) with strict user-configured constraints on maximum slippage, position sizing ceilings, and stop-outs.
* **Automated Fail-Safe Protection**: Any out-of-band market condition (such as anomalous slippage, sudden pool liquidity dry-up, or rebalancing across unwhitelisted pairs) immediately pauses execution and requires explicit one-click user re-authorization.
* **Console View**: Displays live order book fills, pair analytics, slippage parameters, and stop-loss guards.

### 2. Automated Protocol Yield Suite (Yield Farmer)
* **Multi-Vault Staking**: Deploys capital into non-custodial decentralized finance protocols, including Aave v3 Prime (Base), Kamino Automated Yield (Solana), Marinade Liquid Staking (Solana), and Curve Stability Pool (Arbitrum).
* **5-Minute Compounding Epochs**: Executes automated harvest cycles every 5 minutes (288 cycles per 24 hours).
* **Flexible Yield Routing**: Toggle between continuous auto-compounding into your staking principal or receiving daily cash earnings credited to your USD balance.
* **Telemetry Daemon & Epoch Ledger**: Provides transparent logs of oracle price syncs, collateral health checks, and on-chain harvest settlements.
* **Protected Principal Floor**: Staking principal remains preserved with monotonic yield accumulation delivering 0.5% to 0.8% daily.

## Autonomous execution and agent lifecycle

BlackQuant is built entirely around autonomous algorithmic execution: **the platform features no manual trading, chart timing, or manual order placement.** Sizing calculations, pair selections, order routing, and exit timings are decided entirely by the Smart Executor based on quantitative statistical models.

### How to run a Smart Executor

1. **Allocate capital**: Select your strategy suite (Yield Farmer, Liquidity Optimizer, or MEV Arbitrage) and specify the starting capital balance you wish to deploy.
2. **Enable the agent**: Click **Start Executor**. The Smart Executor initializes on live market prices with target execution win rate parameters (e.g. 80% target execution win rate).
3. **Autonomous 24/7 execution**: The Smart Executor actively monitors order books and liquidity pools, detects optimal routes, executes systematic trades, rebalances liquidity ranges, and carries out 5-minute compounding harvest epochs.
4. **Live telemetry and control**: Inspect live fills, open positions, and harvest records in real time on this screen. You retain full non-custodial authority and can pause or stop the Smart Executor with a single click at any time, returning all available equity immediately to your balance.

<Info>
Smart Executors operate with mathematical discipline within your defined risk parameters. You do not need to sit at a screen, watch live price candles, or manually approve individual trade entries: the agent executes autonomously around the clock.
</Info>

## Reading the equity curve

The equity curve plots **account equity over time**, reflecting cumulative deposits, withdrawals, and net trading results. A vertical increase that coincides with a deposit reflects new capital rather than strategy profits.

To isolate strategy performance, consult the dedicated P&L figures and harvest earnings cards rather than the raw equity curve alone.

## Performance distribution by asset

Asset performance breakdown highlights two key risk metrics:

* **Concentration**: Verifies whether returns are distributed evenly or dependent on a single asset.
* **Consistency**: Verifies whether an executor performs reliably across multiple market environments.

## On-chain settlement and verification

All Smart Executor actions settle on decentralized blockchains. You can cross-reference transactions against native block explorers to confirm smart contract execution independently.

<Info>
Independent verification is a foundational principle of decentralized execution. If any dashboard metric appears inconsistent with on-chain state, the blockchain record is the ultimate source of truth.
</Info>
