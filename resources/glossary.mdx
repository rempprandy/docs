---
title: "Glossary"
description: "A to Z glossary of technical terms, quant concepts, and platform features."
---

**Account balance**: A USD figure held against your account, derived from your transaction ledger and used to fund plans, add-ons, and smart executor allocations. See [The custody model](/security/custody-model#where-the-account-balance-fits).

**Active Arbitrage Suite**: High-frequency execution engines (Liquidity Optimizer and MEV Arbitrage) that dynamically rebalance liquidity pools and capture cross-market DEX spreads.

**Add-on**: A permanent account entitlement purchased once with no expiration date. See [Add-ons](/billing/add-ons).

**Automated Protocol Yield Suite**: Decentralized staking engine (Yield Farmer) that deploys capital into vetted protocol vaults with automated 5-minute compounding epochs.

**Basis point (bps)**: One-hundredth of a percentage point (0.01%). For example, 500 bps equals 5%. Calculations use basis points to prevent floating-point rounding errors.

**Calibrated rate**: The historical accuracy of a strategy's signals, verified against resolved on-chain outcomes.

**Compounding Epoch**: A 5-minute automated harvest cycle (288 epochs daily) executed by the Yield Farmer to claim rewards and restake into principal or pay out daily cash.

**Confirmation threshold**: The number of blockchain confirmations required before a deposit is credited to the ledger. Ranges from 1 block on XRP Ledger to 32 blocks on Solana.

**Credible interval**: A Bayesian statistical range containing the true win rate with 90% confidence, expressed as `[low, high]`.

**Declared rate**: An unverified claim stated by an algorithm before resolution. It is never treated as verified performance.

**Deposit address**: A dedicated blockchain address provisioned for a single cryptocurrency on a fixed network.

**Deposit state**: The operational status of a transfer (`WAITING`, `CONFIRMING`, `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `FAILED`, or `EXPIRED`).

**Entitlement**: A programmatic permission granting specific platform capabilities, provided temporarily by subscription plans or permanently by add-ons.

**Extra ID (Memo / Tag)**: An additional destination identifier required on certain blockchains (such as XRP Ledger) to route funds to your account.

**IPN (Instant Payment Notification)**: A cryptographically signed webhook callback from the payment gateway to credit account ledgers. See [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks).

**KYC (Know Your Customer)**: Identity verification required prior to processing capital withdrawals.

**Ledger entry**: An immutable database record representing an individual financial debit or credit with an audit trail.

**Liquidity Optimizer**: An Active Arbitrage executor designed to rebalance liquidity positions and capture range fees across decentralized exchanges.

**MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)**: Value captured by optimizing transaction sequencing, routing, and pool arbitrage within blocks.

**MEV Arbitrage**: A high-frequency Smart Executor that captures cross-market price discrepancies across decentralized exchange pools.

**Non-custodial**: Architectural model where smart contracts can execute designated trades or vault deposits but have no authorization to withdraw user capital to external addresses.

**Non-Custodial Reserve Verification**: Cryptographic validation confirming that assets deployed in Smart Executors are held in audited smart contract vaults on-chain.

**Position**: An active or settled trade or protocol allocation. Concurrent position limits are set by your plan tier.

**Profit factor**: Gross profits divided by gross losses. Published as `null` when no losses have been recorded.

**Recovery code**: Single-use cryptographic backup codes provided for account access if your 2FA authenticator is unavailable.

**Referral code**: A unique eight-character affiliate code used to track referrals and attribute commissions.

**Referral tier**: The commission level awarded on purchases: Tier 1 (direct) pays 5% and Tier 2 (upstream) pays 2%.

**Signal**: A published mathematical observation detecting a market inefficiency or pricing gap. Not an order.

**Signal plan**: A subscription granting real-time feed access and unlocking Smart Executors for 30 or 365 days.

**Smart Executor**: An automated quantitative runtime that executes algorithmic trading or protocol yield strategies within strict risk parameters.

**SSE (Server-Sent Events)**: Real-time unidirectional web stream pushing deposit events and market telemetry to the client without polling.

**TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password)**: Rotating six-digit authentication codes used for two-factor security.

**Win rate**: The proportion of resolved trades that achieved profitability. Published as `null` until trades resolve.

**Yield Farmer**: An automated Smart Executor that allocates capital across vetted DeFi protocols (Aave, Kamino, Marinade, Curve) on 5-minute compounding epochs.
