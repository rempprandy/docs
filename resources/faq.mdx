## Corporate, Legal & Regulatory

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="What legal entity operates BlackQuant?">

<Accordion title="What is BlackQuant's regulatory classification?">
BlackQuant operates as non-custodial financial technology and execution infrastructure. The platform does not take custody of user assets, pool customer funds, or operate as a centralized exchange. Users retain sole ownership of their self-custodied wallets and deploy capital into audited smart contract vaults.

BlackQuant Labs LLC maintains corporate compliance in Delaware and conducts ongoing regulatory reviews across the United Kingdom and Europe, including active alignment with the European Union's **MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)** regulatory framework.
</Accordion>

Each audit includes both a technical specification and a plain-English summary. See [Audit reports](/security/audit-reports).

## Account & Security

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I need to verify my identity to use the platform?">
Identity verification is only required when requesting capital withdrawals. Depositing funds, activating subscription plans, accessing signals, and running Smart Executors do not require upfront verification. However, completing verification early ensures seamless withdrawals later. See [Verify your identity](/getting-started/verify-your-identity).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I signed up with GitHub. Why doesn't my password work?">
Accounts created through GitHub authentication do not have a password set by default. The system returns an authentication failure to preserve security. You can set a password at any time via **Personal → Reset Credentials**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my referral sponsor?">
No. Referral links are permanently bound at the moment of account creation and cannot be transferred or modified.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete my account?">
Yes. Navigate to **My Profile → Delete account**. This permanently purges your personal profile, active sessions, and verification records. We recommend exporting your transaction history beforehand.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can BlackQuant move or withdraw my funds?">
No. For on-chain execution, smart contracts are granted permission to route trades or deposit into vetted yield vaults, but have no code path to withdraw funds to unauthorized addresses. Your USD dashboard balance is stored in an audited internal ledger for plan activations and staking allocations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I lost access to my authenticator device and recovery codes.">
Please contact the [Help Desk](/platform/knowledge-and-help). Manual identity verification will be required to confirm account ownership before restoring access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does BlackQuant support ever ask for my password or 2FA codes?">
Never. Platform representatives will never request your password, 2FA codes, recovery keys, or wallet private keys. Anyone asking for these credentials is an impersonator.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
## Smart Executors & Strategies

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Do I need to manage trades or make market decisions manually?">
No. BlackQuant is 100% automated algorithmic execution: the user does not choose trade directions, analyze candlestick charts, or place orders. You simply allocate your desired capital and enable the Smart Executor agent. The agent's quantitative models handle all market monitoring, entry timing, position sizing, route optimization, exit closures, and yield compounding autonomously around the clock.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Active Arbitrage and Protocol Yield?">
BlackQuant provides two distinct execution suites:
* **Active Arbitrage Suite (Liquidity Optimizer & MEV Arbitrage)**: High-frequency execution Smart Executors that actively enter and exit positions on decentralized exchange order books and AMM pools to capture cross-market spreads and rebalance liquidity ranges. MEV Arbitrage represents our senior HFT execution strategy.
* **Automated Protocol Yield Suite (Yield Farmer)**: A passive capital allocation Smart Executor that stakes capital across vetted decentralized finance protocols (Aave, Kamino, Marinade, Curve), harvesting and restaking rewards on automated 5-minute epochs.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does the Yield Farmer work and what returns does it generate?">
The Yield Farmer distributes staked capital across four audited decentralized protocols: Aave v3 Prime on Base (lending), Kamino Automated Yield on Solana (concentrated liquidity), Marinade & Jito on Solana (liquid staking), and Curve Stability Pool on Arbitrum (stable AMM).

It operates on continuous 5-minute compounding epochs (288 cycles per day), delivering daily yields between 0.1% and 0.3%. Staked capital is safeguarded by non-custodial smart contracts audited by OpenZeppelin and Trail of Bits with cryptographic reserve verification.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which subscription tier unlocks each Smart Executor?">
* **Starter ($29/month)**: Unlocks the Protocol Yield Suite (Yield Farmer) with 1 concurrent execution slot and $1,000 to $10,000 staking capacity.
* **Growth ($79/month)**: Unlocks the Active Arbitrage Suite (Liquidity Optimizer) alongside Yield Farmer, supporting up to 5 concurrent executors and $50,000 staking capacity.
* **Elite ($199/month)**: Unlocks the complete suite including MEV Arbitrage, with unlimited execution slots and up to $250,000 staking capacity.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch between auto-compounding and daily payouts?">
Yes. The Yield Farmer dashboard includes an instant Compounding Mode toggle. You can choose to automatically reinvest harvested yield back into your principal to maximize compound growth, or receive daily cash earnings directly credited to your available USD account balance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the automated fail-safe protection on Smart Executors?">
Smart Executors operate under delegated session keys (ERC-4337) with strict user-defined risk limits. If an out-of-band market anomaly occurs (such as abnormal pool slippage, sudden liquidity dry-up, or unwhitelisted pair rebalancing), execution halts immediately and requires explicit one-click user re-authorization.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
## Deposits & Treasury

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Is my USD account balance the same as my decentralized wallet capital?">
No. Your USD account balance is an internal ledger balance used to activate plans, purchase permanent add-ons, or stake into Smart Executors. Decentralized trading capital resides on-chain under audited smart contract rules. See [The custody model](/security/custody-model).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I transferred funds on the wrong blockchain network. Can they be recovered?">
Transactions sent over unsupported or incorrect networks cannot be recovered by BlackQuant, payment gateways, or blockchain validators. To prevent this, our deposit interface assigns fixed networks per asset rather than using generic dropdowns.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My deposit confirmed on-chain but my balance has not updated.">
Ledger crediting occurs as soon as the cryptographically signed webhook callback is verified. Occasionally, network propagation delays between validators and gateways can take a few minutes. Check [Troubleshooting](/resources/troubleshooting#deposit-states) for detailed state descriptions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is there a minimum deposit requirement?">
Minimum thresholds ensure that transaction sizes remain economically viable relative to underlying blockchain gas fees and payment gateway processing costs.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signals & Quantitative Analytics

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why does a newly listed strategy display null instead of a win rate?">
Strategies display `null` until their initial trades resolve. BlackQuant never substitutes unmeasured strategies with 0%, ensuring untested algorithms are not confused with failing ones.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does a credible interval like 75% [45, 93] mean?">
It indicates that the historical observed win rate is 75%, and there is 90% statistical probability that the true long-term rate falls between 45% and 93%. A broad interval indicates early sample sizes, whereas a narrow interval (such as `75% [72, 78]`) reflects high statistical confidence. See [Reading the numbers honestly](/platform/signal-engine#reading-the-numbers-honestly).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the Signal Engine trade autonomously without user configuration?">
No. The Signal Engine is architecturally separated from order placement. It measures feeds and publishes statistical insights. Trades only execute when you configure manual trades or launch an authorized Smart Executor.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
