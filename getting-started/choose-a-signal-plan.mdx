Navigate to **Apps → Signal Plan**.

A signal plan is a **fixed-term subscription** that provides real-time access to the Signal Engine and unlocks automated Smart Executors. Without an active subscription, live feeds and executor provisioning remain inactive.

## The tiers and capabilities

| Feature | Starter | Growth (Most Popular) | Elite (Pro) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly Price** | Set in Backend (Default: $29) | Set in Backend (Default: $79) | Set in Backend (Default: $199) |
| **Annual Price** | Set in Backend (Default: $276) | Set in Backend (Default: $756) | Set in Backend (Default: $1,908) |
| Signals per day | Up to 10 | Up to 50 | Unlimited |
| Active positions / executors | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Yield Farmer** (Protocol Yield) | Included | Included | Included |
| **Liquidity Optimizer** (Active Arbitrage) | Locked | Included | Included |
| **MEV Arbitrage** (Active Arbitrage) | Locked | Locked | Included |
| Staking range per executor | Set in Backend (e.g. $200 to $1,000,000) | Set in Backend (e.g. $2,000 to $5,000,000) | Set in Backend (e.g. $10,000 to $250,000,000) |
| Notifications | Email | SMS & email | Priority push routing |
| Analytics | Essential | Advanced intervals | Full institutional suite |
| Referral rewards | No | Enabled | Enabled |
| API access | No | No | Full REST & WebSocket |

* **Starter**: For individuals beginning with automated DeFi yield strategies (Yield Farmer).
* **Growth**: For active traders scaling multi-pool DEX arbitrage and liquidity rebalancing (Liquidity Optimizer).
* **Elite**: For professional quant teams requiring high-frequency execution and full MEV Arbitrage routing.

## Monthly versus annual billing

Annual subscriptions offer significant savings (approximately 20% across all tiers) billed as a single 365-day term.

| Tier | Monthly Equivalent × 12 | Annual Price | Annual Savings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $348 | $276 | $72 (21%) |
| Growth | $948 | $756 | $192 (20%) |
| Elite | $2,388 | $1,908 | $480 (20%) |

<Info>
Subscriptions operate on fixed terms (30 days for monthly or 365 days for annual) without unexpected auto-renewals. We do not store payment card details; you choose when to renew upon term conclusion.
</Info>

## How to activate a plan

1. Open **Signal Plan** in the dashboard and select your preferred tier and billing duration.
2. Pay from your **USD account balance**, or initiate an on-demand **cryptocurrency checkout** if your balance is insufficient. See [Paying with crypto](/billing/paying-with-crypto).
3. Your plan entitlements and Smart Executors activate instantly upon payment settlement.

<Info>
Prices are validated server-side from immutable item identifiers. The browser never submits a numerical payment amount, ensuring full security and price integrity.
</Info>

## Upgrading an existing plan

You can upgrade to a higher tier at any point during your subscription. Upgrading unlocks additional executor slots and advanced strategies immediately, initiating a new subscription term.

## What happens upon expiration

When a term concludes, tier-specific executor permissions and live signal streams pause until renewal. Your account balance, deposited assets, referral rewards, and verification records remain completely unaffected.
