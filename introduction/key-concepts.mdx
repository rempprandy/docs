This page outlines the core concepts and vocabulary used throughout BlackQuant and its documentation. For an alphabetical reference, see the [Glossary](/resources/glossary).

## Smart Executors

Automated quantitative runtimes that execute algorithmic strategies on your behalf within defined capital limits and risk boundaries. Smart Executors operate across two specialized suites:

* **Active Arbitrage Suite**: High-frequency execution bots (Liquidity Optimizer and MEV Arbitrage) that rebalance liquidity pool ranges and capture cross-DEX pricing spreads.
* **Automated Protocol Yield Suite**: Passive yield bots (Yield Farmer) that deploy capital into audited decentralized finance vaults and execute automated 5-minute compounding epochs.

## Protocol Yield Engine

The decentralized capital allocation system behind the Yield Farmer. Capital is distributed across vetted DeFi protocols (Aave v3 Prime on Base, Kamino Automated Yield on Solana, Marinade Liquid Staking on Solana, and Curve Stability Pool on Arbitrum) targeting daily yield between 0.1% and 0.3% (~5.2% to 7.4% APY).

## Compounding Epoch

A scheduled 5-minute automated harvest interval executed by the Protocol Yield Engine (running 288 epochs every 24-hour day). During each epoch, protocol rewards are claimed and either auto-compounded into the staking principal to accelerate yield or routed to daily cash payouts.

## Non-Custodial Reserve Verification

Cryptographic verification ensuring that funds deployed into Smart Executors are held directly inside audited smart contract vaults on-chain rather than pooled in an opaque internal reserve. All smart contract foundations have been reviewed by external security firms including OpenZeppelin and Trail of Bits.

## Account balance

A USD ledger figure stored against your BlackQuant account, utilized for activating subscription plans, unlocking add-ons, or allocating to Smart Executors. The balance is derived directly from an immutable transaction ledger rather than an arbitrary database counter.

<Warning>
Your USD account balance is distinct from on-chain decentralized assets held in non-custodial wallets. When you deposit digital assets, they are credited as a USD balance upon reaching confirmation thresholds. See [The custody model](/security/custody-model).
</Warning>

## Ledger entry

An immutable audit record documenting every monetary movement on the platform: an amount in USD, a `kind` category describing the transaction type, and an associated reference identifier. Deposits credit the balance, subscription activations debit the balance, and referral rewards credit the balance.

## Deposit address

A dedicated blockchain address provisioned for a specific asset on a fixed decentralized network. Each address has a minimum deposit threshold to ensure incoming transactions exceed necessary on-chain gas costs.

Certain networks (such as XRP Ledger) require an additional memo or destination tag. Omitting the tag prevents automatic ledger attribution.

## Deposit state

The lifecycle of a cryptocurrency deposit:

| State | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `WAITING` | Address generated; waiting for transaction broadcast on-chain |
| `CONFIRMING` | Transaction detected in mempool; block confirmations accumulating |
| `CONFIRMED` | Confirmation threshold satisfied; balance credited to account ledger |
| `PARTIALLY_PAID` | Transaction value arrived below the expected invoice threshold |
| `FAILED` | Network transaction was dropped, replaced, or encountered an execution failure |
| `EXPIRED` | Payment window closed before the transaction broadcasted |

See [Troubleshooting](/resources/troubleshooting#deposit-states) for handling abnormal deposit states.

## Signal

An analytical data event indicating a detected pricing discrepancy, order book imbalance, or statistical anomaly. A signal is not an order. The Signal Engine has no direct authority to execute trades without user configuration or Smart Executor routing.

## Credible interval

A Bayesian statistical range that contains the true historical win rate with 90% probability, presented as `[low, high]`.

This metric is vital for evaluating strategy reliability. A strategy displaying `75% [45, 93]` has experienced few historical occurrences and carries broad uncertainty, whereas a strategy showing `75% [72, 78]` has resolved hundreds of observations with tight statistical confidence.

<Info>
A performance rate listed as `null` indicates that outcomes are still in progress and not yet measured. The platform never defaults unmeasured results to 0%, preventing untested strategies from being misidentified as failing ones.
</Info>

## Signal plan

A recurring access tier granting access to real-time signals and unlocking specific Smart Executors. Plans run on fixed calendar terms (30 days for monthly or 365 days for annual) without unexpected auto-renewals. Tier details are available in [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing).

## Add-on

A permanent account capability purchased once with no expiration date. Add-ons include additional concurrent position slots, custom webhooks, or automated risk guards. See [Add-ons](/billing/add-ons).

## Entitlement

The programmatic permission granting your account specific access levels across platform services. Plans grant entitlements for fixed subscription durations, while add-ons grant entitlements indefinitely. Permissions are validated dynamically against real-time server timestamps.

## Referral tier

The multi-level distribution model applied to platform purchases:

| Tier | Beneficiary | Commission Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Direct referring affiliate | 5% (500 basis points) |
| Tier 2 | Second-tier upstream affiliate | 2% (200 basis points) |

All commission calculations utilize integer basis points to prevent floating-point rounding errors.

## Referral code

A unique eight-character affiliate identifier generated using a disambiguated character set (omitting easily confused characters like `0`, `o`, `1`, `l`, and `i`). Referral attribution is persisted via browser cookies for 30 days.

## KYC submission

An identity verification document review consisting of identity document images, a liveness verification check, and a verification state (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, or `REJECTED`). Required exclusively prior to capital withdrawals. See [Verify your identity](/getting-started/verify-your-identity).

## Recovery code

Single-use cryptographic fallback codes provided during two-factor authentication setup. Utilizing a recovery code automatically invalidates that specific token in a single atomic database query. See [Account recovery](/security/account-recovery).
