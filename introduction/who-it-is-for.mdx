## BlackQuant is designed for you if

**You prioritize empirical data over marketing hype.** Every performance metric across BlackQuant carries a credible interval, and unresolved strategies remain `null`. If statistical transparency is what you require, the platform architecture will align with your standards.

**You value non-custodial asset control.** The [custody model](/security/custody-model) guarantees that you maintain ownership of your assets. Smart contracts can only execute designated routing and vault allocations, eliminating third-party custody risks.

**You utilize automated quantitative tools.** Whether you run high-frequency DEX arbitrage (Active Arbitrage Suite) or compound passive DeFi yield across audited protocols (Yield Farmer), the platform provides automated, verifiable execution.

**You are comfortable verifying on-chain activity.** All trades, vault allocations, and harvest cycles settle on-chain, allowing you to independently confirm execution via block explorers.

**You understand statistical intervals.** Our metrics show authentic market behavior: wide intervals indicate early-stage sampling, while narrow intervals indicate established data.

## It is not designed for you if

**You are seeking a managed investment fund.** BlackQuant is software and execution infrastructure, not a discretionary asset manager. You configure parameters, choose strategies, and initiate executors.

**You expect guaranteed returns.** All trading and decentralized yield activities involve inherent market volatility and protocol risk. Historical performance and protocol APYs are past observations, not guarantees.

**You prefer custodial third-party handling.** In a non-custodial environment, you manage your own private keys. No support desk can restore lost private keys, which is the direct foundation of self-sovereign asset safety.

**You operate in restricted jurisdictions.** Full account withdrawals require identity verification under standard regulatory guidelines.

## Experience level

| Competency | Practical Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Blockchain Transactions** | Understanding network selection (for example, recognizing that TRC-20 and ERC-20 networks are incompatible). |
| **Statistical Literacy** | Understanding why a metric like `75% [45, 93]` has broader uncertainty than `75% [72, 78]`. |
| **On-Chain Verification** | Basic familiarity with block explorers to verify smart executor transactions and vault deposits. |
| **Technical Development** | Not required. Advanced API and webhook features are entirely optional. |

## Recommended onboarding checklist

1. Review [The custody model](/security/custody-model) to understand non-custodial boundaries.
2. Review [Audit reports](/security/audit-reports) from OpenZeppelin and Trail of Bits covering core contracts.
3. Review [Reading the numbers honestly](/platform/signal-engine#reading-the-numbers-honestly).
4. Fund your account with an initial testing allocation to experience the end-to-end loop (deposit, plan selection, smart executor run, and withdrawal) before scaling capital.

<Warning>
Testing the full operational loop first ensures your identity verification and wallet configuration are confirmed before committing larger allocations.
</Warning>
