## 1. Trail of Bits: Trading Vault and Settlement Review

### Plain-English Summary

* **What exactly was audited?** The core on-chain vault mechanics and automated trade settlement contracts that handle capital allocation.
* **Who performed the audit, and when?** Trail of Bits, conducted April 20 to 24, 2026, and officially published on May 11, 2026.
* **What were the principal findings?** The review identified 1 medium-severity item and 1 low-severity observation concerning edge-case balance accounting and fee rounding. Zero critical or high-severity vulnerabilities were found.
* **Have identified issues been resolved?** Yes. All findings were remediated and verified by the Trail of Bits team before production deployment.
* **What remains outside the scope?** Off-chain server infrastructure, cloud deployment scripts, and external third-party decentralized exchange oracles were out of scope.
* **What this means for your funds:** Validates that core vault accounting, balance checks, and redemption mechanics adhere to expected security specifications under evaluated conditions, substantially mitigating risks of unauthorized extraction or state corruption within audited modules.

## 2. OpenZeppelin: Smart Contract Security Assessment

### Plain-English Summary

* **What exactly was audited?** The foundational smart contract building blocks, including token transfer safety, access control permissions, reentrancy guards, and cross-chain messaging primitives.
* **Who performed the audit, and when?** OpenZeppelin, conducted January 26 to February 5, 2026, and officially published on February 27, 2026.
* **What were the principal findings?** Zero critical and zero high-severity issues. The review highlighted 2 medium-severity, 10 low-severity, and 5 informational observations related to parameter bounds and cross-chain messaging edge cases.
* **Have identified issues been resolved?** Yes. 15 items were fully resolved and 2 were verified with approved operational mitigations.
* **What remains outside the scope?** Front-end web interfaces, user-side wallets, and third-party RPC endpoints were excluded.
* **What this means for your funds:** Verifies that our foundational contracts prevent unauthorized administrative takeovers, enforce strict access controls, and guarantee that smart contract routes cannot execute arbitrary fund transfers.

## How to evaluate an audit report objectively

1. **Verify the audited commit hash:** Ensure the code running in production matches the exact repository commit reviewed by the auditing firm.
2. **Review the testing scope:** Examine what parts of the system were included and what components were excluded (such as off-chain relays or UI code).
3. **Inspect the remediation log:** A healthy security review uncovers findings. The crucial factor is that all identified issues were addressed, re-tested, and verified by external auditors before live deployment.
4. **Recognize audit limits:** An audit is a rigorous point-in-time assessment of code correctness; it does not replace ongoing risk management, automated stop-outs, or personal wallet security.
