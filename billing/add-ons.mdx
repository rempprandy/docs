Add-ons are offered on the store surface alongside the deposit flow, separately from standard [signal plans](/billing/plans-and-pricing). They provide modular account expansions with transparent, upfront pricing.

## Permanent add-ons

Purchased once, owned indefinitely. No expiration dates, no renewals.

| Add-on | Price | Description | Item ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **VIP Analytics Pack** | $200 | Advanced attribution and Bayesian drawdown reporting | `vip-analytics` |
| **Priority Support** | $150 | Guaranteed 4-hour response SLA, around the clock | `priority-support` |
| **Extra Positions Slot** | $100 | Adds one concurrent position or Smart Executor slot | `extra-position-slot` |
| **Risk Guard Add-on** | $250 | Automated on-chain stop-out at a customized drawdown threshold | `risk-guard` |

<Info>
Permanent add-ons carry **no expiration timestamp**. Once acquired, the capability remains permanently linked to your account profile.
</Info>

## Signal Pro: subscription add-on

Signal Pro is positioned within the add-on catalog but operates as a subscription term with fixed renewal cycles:

| Period | Price | Term Duration | Item ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $500 | 30 days | `signal-pro-monthly` |
| Annual | $4,800 | 365 days | `signal-pro-annual` |

*Automated signals, rebalanced daily.* Annual billing includes **two months free** compared to monthly renewals ($4,800 versus $6,000 equivalent).

## Selecting the right add-ons

* **Extra Positions Slot**: Valuable for accounts on Starter (1 slot) or Growth (5 slots) seeking higher executor concurrency without upgrading their entire plan tier. Elite includes unlimited slots by default.
* **VIP Analytics Pack**: Unlocks deep statistical modeling and interval breakdowns for accounts on the Starter tier.
* **Priority Support**: Provides 24/7 accelerated support for all tiers (Elite accounts receive a dedicated account manager separately).
* **Risk Guard**: Integrates automated risk control directly into execution runtimes across all tiers.

<Warning>
Review your current plan's included entitlements in [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing) prior to purchasing an add-on to avoid duplicating features already granted.
</Warning>

## How to purchase an add-on

1. Pay from your unencumbered **USD account balance**, or initiate an on-demand **cryptocurrency checkout**.
2. The entitlement activates instantly upon payment settlement.
3. Duplicate purchases of active permanent add-ons are prevented with an informative alert.

Prices are looked up server-side from immutable item identifiers: the client browser never submits an arbitrary payment figure.

## Referral rewards

Add-on purchases disburse referral commissions identically to subscription plans: 5% to Tier 1 and 2% to Tier 2. Learn more in [Referral Hub](/platform/referral-hub).
