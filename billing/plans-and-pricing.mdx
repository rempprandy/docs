## Server-side price verification

Plan prices and operational parameters are defined dynamically in the backend catalog (`plans.catalogue`) and database. When an account activates or renews a subscription, the client submits an immutable **item ID** (`plan-starter-monthly`, `plan-growth-monthly`, etc.). The backend verifies the price directly against the server-side catalog before executing the balance debit or generating an on-chain crypto checkout invoice.

<Info>
Prices and capital constraints are never accepted from the client browser. All checkout totals and term durations are enforced server-side by backend validation, preserving institutional integrity.
</Info>

## Multi-tier referral rewards

Active Growth and Elite subscribers earn multi-tier referral rewards on downstream subscription activations across their affiliate tree: **5% to Tier 1** and **2% to Tier 2**, calculated directly from the purchase price. Referral rewards are funded directly from platform margins, not deducted from your balance. Learn more in [Referral Hub](/platform/referral-hub).
