## Server-side price verification

Plan prices are stored securely in the backend catalog. When you activate a subscription, the client submits an immutable **item ID** and the server verifies the current cost directly from the database.

<Info>
Prices cannot be altered on the client side. The browser never submits a numerical payment amount, preserving transaction integrity.
</Info>

## Subscriptions versus permanent add-ons

| Attribute | Subscription Plan | Permanent Add-on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed Term Duration | Yes (30 or 365 days) | No (Permanent entitlement) |
| Expiration Date | Yes | Never |
| Auto-Renewal Requirement | No (Manual renewal) | Not applicable |
| Re-purchasable While Held | No | No |

See [Add-ons](/billing/add-ons) for permanent account upgrades such as extra position slots and custom risk guards.

## Multi-tier referral rewards

Every subscription activation awards referral rewards up the affiliate tree: **5% to Tier 1** and **2% to Tier 2**, calculated directly from the purchase price. Referral rewards are funded directly from platform margins, not deducted from your balance. Learn more in [Referral Hub](/platform/referral-hub).
